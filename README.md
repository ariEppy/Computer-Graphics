# Computer-Graphics

This is a computer graphics project that shows the process of erosion. It was written in C++.

You can see a demo of the erosion here - https://drive.google.com/file/d/1IMUX-z_NCCKT-FATGD9lgcceqYUbIl9-/view?usp=drive_link

You can move backwards and forwards, left and right. Press the right button and then erosion to see the ground erode. Press the right button again and press place city to form a city on the ground. 

Cities can only appear in grounds that are within a close proximity to both the sea and a river.
